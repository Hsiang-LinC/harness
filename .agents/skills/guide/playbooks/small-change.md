**One ticket, one record, one PR. Prepare makes the record; Execute delivers it.**

1. Without a bound record, [Prepare](../procedures/prepare.md), with `entryMethod: "implement"` in the record.
2. [Execute](../procedures/execute.md) the ticket. For an open PR in this session's hands (review comments, red checks, "ship it"), Execute resumes at the step `harness work read` shows.
3. A finding outside the record, or a decision only a person can make: go to Scope change.

**Exit** when Execute's exit holds.

**Reply:** Execute's reply.
